# 12 — Adoption and Measuring Impact

## Learning objectives

- Build an adoption plan that addresses behavior and operating model.
- Measure outcomes rather than activity alone.
- Create a feedback loop for continuous improvement.

## Adoption sequence

1. Establish baseline delivery and quality measures.
2. Select a narrow, representative pilot.
3. Agree on success criteria and guardrails.
4. Train a small group through hands-on exercises.
5. Capture friction, failure modes, and reusable patterns.
6. Improve templates and workflows.
7. Expand through champions, office hours, and team-level coaching.
8. Review impact and risks regularly.

## Metrics

**Flow:** lead time, cycle time, task-to-production time, deployment frequency.

**Quality and stability:** change failure rate, escaped defects, recovery time, flaky test rate.

**Adoption:** active usage by workflow, repeat usage, enablement participation, workflow completion.

**Efficiency:** time saved validated by sampling, rework, agent cost per accepted outcome.

Do not optimize for prompt count, lines of generated code, or raw agent usage. Pair speed metrics with quality and sustainability measures.

## Practical exercise

Create a 30/60/90-day enablement plan using the [adoption playbook](../examples/adoption/adoption-playbook.md). Choose three outcome metrics and define how each will be measured.

## Expected outcome

A practical rollout plan with owners, baseline, target, guardrails, and review cadence.
