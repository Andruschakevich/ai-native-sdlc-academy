# AI Engineering Adoption Playbook

## 1. Establish the baseline
Measure current workflow performance and quality. Document data sources, definitions, and sample size.

## 2. Choose the pilot
Select a frequent, bounded workflow with clear acceptance criteria, low blast radius, and fast feedback.

## 3. Agree on guardrails
Define approved tools, data handling, access, review, and escalation requirements before enabling the pilot.

## 4. Enable the team
Use short demonstrations, hands-on workshops, pairing, office hours, and reusable examples. Train reviewers as well as tool users.

## 5. Capture evidence
Track accepted outcomes, rework, failure modes, quality signals, and user feedback. Avoid treating raw usage as success.

## 6. Improve and scale
Turn successful patterns into shared templates and skills. Expand only when the workflow is repeatable and risks are understood.

## Example 30/60/90-day plan

| Period | Focus | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Baseline and pilot | Use case, guardrails, baseline, initial training |
| Days 31–60 | Validate and improve | Evaluation results, improved workflow, lessons learned |
| Days 61–90 | Scale responsibly | Enablement kit, champions, metrics review, rollout decision |

## Outcome metrics
- Task cycle time and lead time
- Acceptance rate and rework
- Change failure rate and escaped defects
- Time to recover from failed changes
- Cost per accepted outcome
- Repeat adoption of validated workflows

Always pair speed measures with quality and stability measures.
