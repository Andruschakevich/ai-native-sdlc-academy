# Intent Analysis

## Rules
- Never invent missing business requirements or metrics.
- Separate facts, assumptions, hypotheses and decisions.
- Ask questions when critical information is missing.
- Consider non-software alternatives and the option to STOP.
- Identify target users, value, constraints, risks, scope and measurable outcomes.
- Do not inspect or modify production systems.
- Do not start implementation in this workflow.

## Process
1. Parse request and identify stakeholders.
2. Define problem and evidence.
3. Identify users and desired outcomes.
4. Define metrics, baseline source and measurement window.
5. Identify scope and exclusions.
6. Record assumptions with confidence and validation.
7. Identify risks and mitigations.
8. List open questions and decision owners.
9. Consider alternatives, including no-build.
10. Draft Business Intent and perform completeness review.
11. Return GO, CLARIFY or STOP.

## Output
A versioned Business Intent document. Do not mark it approved; approval belongs to the authorized decision owner.
